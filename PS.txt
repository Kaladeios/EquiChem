3. Quick Formula Reference to Normalize Labels Yourself
If you are plugging numbers into a spreadsheet (like Excel or Google Sheets) to compare multiple ingredients at once, use these standard pharmaceutical conversions to put every ingredient into mg per 1 mL (or 1 g):
% w/v to mg/mL: Multiply the percentage by 10 (e.g., 1% w/v = 1 g per 100 mL = 10 mg/mL).
% w/w to mg/g: Multiply the percentage by 10 (e.g., 2% w/w = 2 g per 100 g = 20 mg/g).
% v/v to mL/mL: Divide the percentage by 100 (e.g., 7% v/v = 0.07 mL of active liquid per 1 mL of medicine). To convert that 0.07 mL into mg, multiply by the ingredient's density (mg/mL).
